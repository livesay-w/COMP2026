# φ⁴ field theory with a convolutional normalizing flow

Sample 2D lattice φ⁴ theory, p(φ) = e^{−S[φ]}/Z, on a periodic L×L lattice (lattice units), with

S[φ] = Σ_x [ ½ Σ_μ (φ_{x+μ} − φ_x)² + ½ m² φ_x² + λ φ_x⁴ ]

using a RealNVP-style flow: checkerboard affine couplings whose scale and shift come from small
3×3 CNNs with circular padding. Train by reverse KL against the action (no data). Make sampling exact with
independence Metropolis. Validate against local Metropolis and the exact free theory. Then work toward the critical point.

Interpreter: `C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe` (torch 2.15 CPU).
